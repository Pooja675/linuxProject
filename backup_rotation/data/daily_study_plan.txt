DAILY STUDY PLAN
================

06:30 - 07:00  Walk or light exercise
07:00 - 08:00  Breakfast and plan the day
09:00 - 11:00  Deep work: one focused coding topic
11:00 - 11:15  Break
11:15 - 13:00  Build or fix something in a project
13:00 - 14:00  Lunch and rest
14:00 - 15:30  Practice problems (DSA / scripting)
15:30 - 16:00  Revise notes
16:00 - 17:00  Read documentation or watch a tutorial
21:30          Write down what was learned today, then sleep

Rule of thumb: finish one small thing every day.
